# Decision title

- **Should I adopt this?** One sentence.
- **Scope:** Answer, persistent harness, or improver actually used in a later round.
- **Change:** Before/after artifacts, pinned sources, and who proposed the change.
- **Protocol:** Model/revision, evaluator, development/selection vs. final splits, tasks, and attempts or seeds.
- **Controls and costs:** Unchanged baseline, comparable-budget extra attempts, and search vs. execution cost. Mark missing facts `unknown`.
- **Results:** Aggregate, uncertainty if available, regressions, and paired counts only when authentic matched outcomes exist.
- **Evidence:** Author-reported, recalculated from original outcomes, or independently run. Note inspected artifacts separately.
- **Limits and next check:** What cannot be concluded, and how to test transfer.
