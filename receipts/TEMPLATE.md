**Claim:** The headline as its authors state it, with the number, the benchmark and the split. Link the versioned source.

**Change:** What was edited between before and after, and by whom or what. Link pinned artifacts where they exist.

**Controls:** The unchanged baseline, any matched-budget baseline, how selection and final tasks were kept apart, and how many runs. Write `unknown` for anything the source does not say.

**Result:** The numbers, including results that went the other way.

**Verified:** Who checked the number and how: the authors, a recalculation from released outcomes, an independent rerun, or an artifact anyone can check. Say what you did yourself.

**Open:** What cannot be concluded from this evidence.

**Try it:** The smallest experiment that would settle the open question on someone's own tasks.
